My research focuses on the resolution of intrastate armed conflict, with particular interests in ceasefires, mediation, negotiation, peace agreement implementation, political violence, and international security. I also conduct research on nuclear disarmament and international responses to emerging disarmament norms.

## Conflict Resolution and Peace Processes

A central strand of my research examines how domestic and international actors shape conflict resolution and the implementation of ceasefire and peace agreements. I investigate the conditions under which different forms of mediation and third-party involvement contribute to sustainable peace.

My work on peace agreement implementation develops the concept of **third-party implementation governance**, focusing on how international, regional, and domestic actors coordinate monitoring, mediation, security guarantees, political leverage, and other implementation functions.

## Ceasefires and Mediation

I study ceasefire negotiations and third-party mediation in intrastate armed conflicts, with particular attention to how different types of mediators influence temporary and definitive ceasefire outcomes.

## Public Opinion and Peace

I examine bottom-up perspectives on conflict resolution and sustainable peace. My current research in Yemen investigates citizens' experiences of conflict, preferences for peace and mediation, and attitudes toward alternative political settlements.

## Nuclear Disarmament

A further strand of my research examines nuclear disarmament and international responses to emerging disarmament norms, including the Treaty on the Prohibition of Nuclear Weapons (TPNW). This work focuses particularly on coercive diplomacy, security partnerships, and variation in states' responses to international disarmament initiatives.

## Methods

My research employs mixed-method designs combining quantitative and qualitative approaches, including cross-national statistical analysis, comparative case studies, process tracing, large-scale surveys, and survey experiments.
